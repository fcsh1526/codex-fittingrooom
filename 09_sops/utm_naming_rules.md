# UTM 命名規則

## 格式

`utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={week}_{bucket}&utm_content={format}_{variant}`

## 欄位規則

| 欄位 | 範例 | 說明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| source | instagram, xiaohongshu, pinterest, tiktok, threads | 平台 |
| medium | social, affiliate, email, line | 流量類型 |
| campaign | w01_commute_capsule | 週次與內容桶 |
| content | carousel_a, pin_b, note_cta_line | 版型與測試版本 |

## 範例

Instagram carousel：
`utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=w01_commute_capsule&utm_content=carousel_a`

小紅書筆記：
`utm_source=xiaohongshu&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=w01_rain_commute&utm_content=note_cta_line`

Pinterest：
`utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=w01_korean_minimal&utm_content=pin_2x3_a`
