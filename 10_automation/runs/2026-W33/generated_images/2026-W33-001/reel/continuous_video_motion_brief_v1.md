# W33-001 Continuous Reel Motion Brief v1

## Purpose

Create one continuous 15-second lifestyle-fashion shot from the accepted Hero A. The clothing must remain the subject from the first frame. This is not a slideshow, morph, montage, or sequence of still-image poses.

## Timeline

### 0.0–3.0 seconds — immediate motion hook

- Continue the walking movement already visible in Hero A.
- She completes one natural step across the lobby, moving slightly toward the exit without approaching the camera.
- Her open windbreaker hem and hair tips respond subtly to the step.
- The cream top, high-waisted navy trousers, shoes, and shoulder bag remain fully readable.

### 3.0–7.0 seconds — garment movement

- She takes one more small step and naturally steadies the shoulder-bag strap once.
- The bag has a small physically plausible bounce and then settles.
- The thin unlined windbreaker stays open; its lightweight fabric folds and relaxes naturally.

### 7.0–11.0 seconds — turn toward the rainy street

- She slows beside the window and turns her upper body about 20–30 degrees toward the rain-damp street.
- Her gaze moves outside; she blinks once naturally.
- Her free arm settles beside her body without posing.

### 11.0–15.0 seconds — quiet ending with full outfit visible

- She shifts her weight naturally onto one leg and lightly smooths one front edge of the open windbreaker once.
- She releases the fabric and keeps looking outside with a calm, attentive expression.
- End with the full outfit still visible and the subject stable enough for a clean loop or cover frame.

## Camera Lock

- One unbroken shot; no cuts, transitions, zooms, reframing, rack focus, slow motion, or speed ramps.
- Fixed full-frame mirrorless-camera viewpoint with a 50mm normal-lens look.
- Chest-height camera and level optical axis.
- Preserve the original 9:16 framing and full-body visibility from hairstyle to both shoes.
- Keep the background geometry, window frame, wall, floor, and rainy street fixed.

## Identity and Wardrobe Lock

- Preserve the exact face, hairstyle, apparent age, body proportions, skin texture, and expression style of accepted Hero A.
- Preserve every garment, color, layer, hem, sleeve, trouser length, shoe, bag, and bag strap exactly.
- The olive windbreaker must remain visibly thin, unlined, matte, airy, open, and summer-appropriate.
- Maintain physically plausible cloth movement, bag weight, foot placement, balance, and contact shadows.

## Avoid

- No face drift, beautification, age change, hairstyle change, body reshaping, elongated legs, or camera-perspective change.
- No extra fingers, merged fingers, changing hand size, floating feet, sliding shoes, or broken joints.
- No bag resizing, strap duplication, strap teleportation, garment redesign, changing trouser fit, changing shoe design, or sudden fabric inflation.
- No thick bomber, coat, knitwear, lining, autumn styling, or winter texture.
- No added people, objects, signs, text, logos, rain indoors, scene change, background warping, or lighting flicker.
- No smiling performance, camera-facing pose, runway walk, dramatic hair flip, or exaggerated model gesture.
