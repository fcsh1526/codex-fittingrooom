## Recommendation

Use W21-P007 as the first publishable post because it has the best mix of identity consistency, outfit clarity, platform fit, and shopping value. The post angle should be "one beige suit, three ways to look polished without looking formal." Pair it with bag, blazer, trousers, camisole, and heel search keywords.

Use W21-P002 as the second post for "buttery neutral office outfit." It is clean and commercially understandable, but it overlaps with W21-P007, so publish it after testing the first office capsule.

Use W21-P006 mainly for Pinterest because the old-stone setting makes it aspirational but less directly tied to Taiwan daily purchase behavior.

## Prompt Feedback

- Keep the current Mika Lin identity block. Face consistency is stable enough for the first two-week test.
- For Taiwan conversion content, ask Grok for "modern Taipei office lobby, department store fitting area, cafe street, MRT-adjacent walkway" instead of European resort scenes.
- For shopping content, add "clear full-body outfit, unobstructed garment structure, realistic fabric seams, no exaggerated runway pose" to every prompt.
