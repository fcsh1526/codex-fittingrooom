## Before Export

- Check that the Canva design still reads as one continuous panorama.
- Check that no text crosses the 1080 / 2160 / 3240 / 4320 boundaries.
- Check that all disclosure text is visible.
- Check that `AI 虛擬穿搭示意` appears at least once.

## Instagram Upload

1. Open Instagram on mobile.
2. Tap `+`.
3. Choose `Post`.
4. Tap `Select multiple`.
5. Select exported images in order from 01 to 05.
6. Do not apply heavy filters.
7. Paste caption.
8. Publish or schedule.
